Paw Pairs Support

A memory matching game for kids on iPhone and iPad

How to play

  1. Tap Lily's Classic for the original game, or Niki's Adventure for the new one: Animals, or State Animals for all 50 states.
  2. Tap a card to turn it over, then tap another one.
  3. If the two cards match, they stay face up. If not, they turn back over. Remember where they were!
  4. Find every pair to win. Your moves and time are shown at the top, and your best times are saved in 🏆 Best Scores.
  5. 👭 2 Players: take turns on one device. Find a pair and you go again; miss and it's the other player's turn.

Questions

Does Paw Pairs need the internet?
No. It works completely offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. Paw Pairs has no ads, no purchases and no links out of the app.

Does the app collect any data?
No. Player names and scores stay on your device. See the privacy policy.

I deleted the app and the scores are gone.
Scores live only on the device, inside the app, so deleting the app deletes them.
