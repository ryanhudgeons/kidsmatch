KidsMatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

KidsMatch is a memory matching game for children, made by Ryan Hudgeons.

The short version: KidsMatch does not collect any information about you or your child. None.

Details

  • No personal information is collected, stored on any server, or shared.
  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not connect to the internet.
  • The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no links to outside websites.
  • The app uses no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising software.
  • Player names and game scores typed into the app are kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere, and they are removed when you delete the app.
  • The app does not access the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or any other data on the device.

Children's privacy

KidsMatch is designed for children. Because the app collects no data at all, no information about a child is ever gathered, stored on a server, or transmitted. This is in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store guidelines for apps in the Kids category.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address and the date at the top will be updated.
